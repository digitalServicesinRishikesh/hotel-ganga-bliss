Guaranteed Reservation

All reservations must be guaranteed with a deposit. Guests must be 18 years or older. We accept bank transfer and cash payments. A 50% deposit is required at the time of booking to guarantee your reservation, with the remaining 50% due at check-in. Please ensure you receive a reservation confirmation number when making your booking. In any case reservations must be cancelled at least 7 days prior to the arrival date (Ghallu Homestay time) to avoid a cancellation fee.


Early Check-In/Pre-Registration

Early check-in is offered based on availability. If you require guaranteed check-in before 12:00 AM, pre-registration and payment are required. Please contact the front desk staff directly to make reservations and complete a payment transfer confirmation In favor of the Ghallu Homestay 's bank account.


Check-In Requirements

Guests must be at least 18 years of age to check in at the Ghallu Homestay . For security reasons and to prevent fraud, guests are required to confirm their identity by providing a valid government-issued photo ID (such as a state driver's license, passport, etc.) at check-in. A payment method in the name of the guest registering is also required. It is the guest's responsibility to fully understand how their bank processes pre-authorizations and charges related to the transaction.


Check-Out Procedure

Check-out time is 10:00 AM. Please check out at the front desk so that housekeeping can begin cleaning your room as soon as possible. If you require a later check-out, please contact the front desk prior to your departure date. We will do our best to accommodate your request, but it is subject to availability. A charge may apply for late check-out.


Special Requests

We will make every effort to honor special requests, such as a specific room number, adjoining rooms, extra beds, etc., upon your arrival. Payments

All reservations and registrations must be guaranteed with payment. All guests are required to present a valid method of payment, even if they plan to pay in cash upon check-out.


Individual room reservation, deposits & guarantees

A deposit is required to make an individual room reservation. However, a valid method of payment is required at the time of booking to guarantee the room and secure the reservation.

Management reserves the right to enter a room with an “IN” status for reasonable purposes, such as in an emergency, for housekeeping, maintenance, to verify that the room, its furnishings, and mechanical equipment are intact, or to address or prevent a violation of our Ghallu Homestay policy/house rules.

Children Policy

Children are welcome, ! Children aged 0 to 4 years stay free when using existing bedding. Please note that children in this age group may not be eligible for complimentary breakfast.
Well-behaved children of all ages are welcome. Children aged 4 and under stay for free when sharing a room with one or more paying adult(s) and using existing bedding. As parents, guardians, or chaperones, you are personally and legally responsible for your children and must supervise them at all times. For safety reasons, please do not leave children unattended in guest rooms or allow them to roam the Ghallu Homestay property unsupervised. Children are not permitted in public areas without being accompanied and supervised by a parent, guardian.


Maximum Occupancy

Room occupancy requirements are based on safety regulations. The maximum occupancy for a standard room is 2 guests. A limited number of extra beds (drawer beds) are available upon request. Availability is on a first-come, first-served basis for all rooms. If the maximum number of guests is exceeded, you will be asked to rent an additional guestroom for proper accommodations or vacate the Ghallu Homestay .


Room Keys

Room keys are issued to the registered guest(s). No room keys will be issued to youth under 18 at any time. I.D. is required if you have lost your key and require a duplicate. Please return room keys to Front Desk at Check-out.


Housekeeping/Room Inspection

Housekeeping is provided daily between 9:00 AM and 2:00 PM, or on demand. Housekeeping may begin as early as necessary, subject to availability. Additional requests, such as extra beds or specific room preferences, will be accommodated based on availability. Rooms are cleaned and inspected daily, and a detailed log is maintained for each room and its linens. Rooms are rented to guests in appropriate condition, free of any prohibited odors. Our housekeeping and front desk staff are trained to identify odors from prohibited items. If our investigation concludes that you have smoked in your room, cooked, or brought a prohibited item into the Ghallu Homestay , you will be fined and evicted without a refund.


Linen Changing

Your comfort is very important to us. For guests staying multiple nights, bed linens are changed on a rotation schedule. Used towels are exchanged for fresh towels daily. If housekeeping is unable to change the bed due to personal items left on it, a note will be left. Housekeeping will be happy to change your bed linens and make your bed each day if all personal items are removed. Please contact our front desk staff if you have any additional questions or concerns.


Lost & Found Policy

We assume no liability for lost, misplaced, stolen, or damaged valuables or belongings. If you discover that you have left behind something of value, please contact us immediately at the front desk, and we will make every effort to assist you in locating your lost item.


Found Items

The Ghallu Homestay is not responsible for any items left behind by a guest. However, any item—except for perishable items—left behind by guests and found by housekeeping after departure will be collected, logged, and stored in a secure location for collection by the owner for up to fourteen (14) days. Records of lost and found items are retained for 6 months. Reasonable efforts will be made to notify the guest if an item is found. Perishable items, underwear, and miscellaneous toiletries will be discarded.


Unclaimed Items/No Contact

Lost and found items are held for fourteen (14) days while we attempt to contact the guest. If the guest's contact information is incorrect or their cell phone mailbox is full, and we are unable to reach the guest during the fourteen (14) day holding period, the unclaimed item(s) will be stored for an additional 1 months. After this period, any unclaimed item(s) will be discarded, donated to local organizations, or disposed of accordingly by the Ghallu Homestay .
